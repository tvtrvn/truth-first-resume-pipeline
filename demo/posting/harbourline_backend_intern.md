# Backend Developer Intern — Harbourline Financial

**Location:** Toronto, ON (hybrid, 3 days in office)
**Term:** Fall 2026 (Sept – Dec), full-time internship
**Team:** Payments Platform
**Posting ID:** HLF-2026-0417

## About Harbourline Financial

Harbourline Financial is a mid-size financial services company serving small
businesses across Canada. The Payments Platform team builds and operates the
internal APIs that move money between our products.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain REST API endpoints in Python used by internal product teams
- Write SQL queries and migrations against our transactional databases
- Containerize services and contribute to our Docker-based local dev environment
- Add tests and keep our CI/CD pipelines green
- Participate in code reviews, standups, and sprint planning with your team

## Required qualifications

- Currently enrolled in a Computer Science or related degree program
- Experience building applications in **Python**
- Understanding of **REST API** design (routes, verbs, status codes)
- Working knowledge of **SQL** (queries, joins, schema design)
- Familiarity with **Docker** or other container tooling
- Clear written communication

## Nice to have

- Exposure to **CI/CD** pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar)
- Experience working on a **team** — group projects, part-time work, clubs
- Interest in financial technology

Submit a resume (PDF, one page preferred) through the Harbourline careers
